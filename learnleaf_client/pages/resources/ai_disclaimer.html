Purpose of the AI Features

LearnLeaf uses artificial intelligence as a learning aid. Its features may explain passages, summarize material, generate revision questions, and create progress-based learning checkpoints. These responses are generated automatically and are intended to support, not direct, your learning process.

How Reading Context Is Used

LearnLeaf may provide the AI with a selected passage, the current chapter, or content from the beginning of a book up to your saved reading position. Cross-book checkpoints may combine the portions reached in several books. Reading positions are approximate, so relevant material may occasionally be omitted or included unintentionally.

Accuracy and Limitations

AI-generated responses may be incorrect, incomplete, outdated, or inconsistent with the source material. The AI may misunderstand a passage or confidently present invented details. LearnLeaf does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of any AI output.

Verify Important Information

Compare important answers, summaries, quotations, and references with the original EPUB and other trustworthy sources. Do not rely solely on an AI response when accuracy matters, and ask a qualified person when you are uncertain about the material.

Educational Use Only

LearnLeaf is designed to supplement personal study. It does not replace the original book, a complete course, an instructor, or independent research. AI-generated quizzes and checkpoints are informal study tools and should not be treated as official assessments or grades.

No Professional Advice

AI responses provided by LearnLeaf are not medical, legal, financial, safety, or other professional advice. Do not use them as a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional or as the sole basis for an important decision.

Data and Third-Party Processing

To generate a response, LearnLeaf may send your prompt and relevant book excerpts to its configured AI provider. Avoid including confidential or sensitive personal information in a prompt. Review the Privacy Policy for more information about how LearnLeaf handles account, reading, and AI-interaction data.

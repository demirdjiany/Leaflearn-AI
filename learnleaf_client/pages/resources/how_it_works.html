How It Works

Step 1

Create Your Account

Create an account so LearnLeaf can keep your folders, uploaded books, reading progress, and AI conversations connected to you. If you already have an account, log in to continue your learning journey.

↓
Step 2

Create a Learning Folder

Create a folder for the subject you want to study, such as HTML, JavaScript, or PHP. Folders keep related EPUB books together and give LearnLeaf a clear topic for future progress summaries.

↓
Step 3

Upload an EPUB

Choose a learning folder and upload an EPUB that you legally own or have permission to use. LearnLeaf will associate the book and its available metadata with that folder.

↓
Step 4

Read and Track Your Progress

Open the EPUB in LearnLeaf's browser reader. Your current reading position and approximate completion percentage can be saved so you can return to the book and continue where you stopped.

↓
Step 5

Ask the AI Companion

Select a difficult passage or ask a question about material you have reached. The AI companion uses the relevant reading context to explain concepts while avoiding content from later in the book where possible.

↓
Step 6

Generate Learning Activities

Ask LearnLeaf for a summary, a simpler explanation, revision questions, or a short quiz based on the material you have completed. These tools help you review instead of only reading passively.

↓
Step 7

Review Your Learning Across Books

When several books belong to the same folder, LearnLeaf can combine the portions you have reached into a short learning checkpoint. This shows the main ideas you should understand across your current reading.

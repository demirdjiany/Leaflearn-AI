Information We Collect

LearnLeaf may collect the account information you provide, such as your name and email address, along with a securely hashed version of your password. The platform also stores your folder and book details, uploaded EPUB files, reading position, completion percentage, and condensed book context created when you use the AI feature.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to manage your account, organize your learning library, restore your reading position, calculate progress, and provide AI assistance based on material you have reached. LearnLeaf does not use this information for advertising or sell it to other parties.

Uploaded Books and Content

EPUB files and their metadata are stored so you can access and read your books through LearnLeaf. You should upload only content that you legally own or have permission to use. Uploaded material is not intended to be made public or shared with other LearnLeaf users without your direction.

AI Processing

When you ask the AI a question, LearnLeaf may send your question, relevant portions of the current book, and condensed context from earlier questions about that book to the configured AI provider. This context is used to help answer questions and is not offered as a full-book or cross-book summary. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in AI prompts, and remember that the provider's own data practices may also apply.

How We Store and Protect Data

LearnLeaf is designed to store account and learning data in its application database and uploaded-book storage. Planned safeguards include password hashing, prepared database statements, upload validation, authenticated sessions, and ownership checks for folders and books. No online service can guarantee complete security, so users should choose a unique password and avoid uploading sensitive material.

Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update your account information and delete individual books or folders using the available controls. Deleting a book or folder also removes its associated reading progress and stored AI context from the active application. Limited copies could remain temporarily in backups or logs where applicable.
